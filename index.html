Biography

Her journey from rising star to global powerhouse.

Born on February 19, 2004, Millie Bobby Brown is a British actress and producer who quickly became one of Hollywood's most prominent young talents. She rose to international fame for her breakout role as Eleven in Netflix’s critically acclaimed science fiction series Stranger Things, earning two Emmy Award nominations.

Beyond Hawkins, Millie has shown her versatility as a leading actress and producer. She starred as the titular character in the hit mystery films Enola Holmes and its sequel, and took on the role of Princess Elodie in the dark fantasy action thriller Damsel.

In 2018, at just 14 years old, she was appointed the youngest-ever UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador, using her global platform to advocate for children's rights, education, and anti-bullying awareness. In addition to her philanthropy and acting, she is the founder of Florence by Mills, a clean and conscious beauty brand designed for Gen-Z.

As UNICEF's youngest-ever Goodwill Ambassador, Millie Bobby Brown uses her global platform to speak out on issues affecting youth worldwide. From addressing world leaders at the United Nations Headquarters to visiting field programs, she remains a passionate advocate for bringing positive change, safety, and empowerment to the next generation.

Her advocacy focuses on ensuring every child has access to quality education, raising awareness around youth mental health, and fighting online bullying and discrimination to establish true gender equality.

Fun Facts

Get answers to the most popular inquiries about Millie's background and projects.

Millie Bobby Brown was born on February 19, 2004, in Marbella, Andalusia, Spain. She is the third of four children born to British parents, Kelly and Robert Brown. The family moved back to the UK when she was four years old and later relocated to Orlando, Florida.

On World Children's Day in November 2018, Millie was appointed as a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador at the age of 14, making her the youngest person ever to receive this title. She uses her voice to address bullying, rights to education, safety, and empowerment.

Florence by Mills is Millie's clean, vegan, and cruelty-free beauty, skincare, and hair care line launched in 2019. Named after her great-grandmother Florence, the brand's philosophy focuses on self-expression, beauty self-care, and toxic-free, accessible ingredients for Gen-Z.
